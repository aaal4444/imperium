          How to play

          Each day brings five realms from history to name, one round at a time, scored out of 500. Each round you begin with only when it was. The chart and the clues do the rest.

          1. Guess straight away for 100 points, or break the next seal for another clue. Each clue lowers the prize: 100, 90, 75, 60, 45, 30, 15.
          2. Clues move the chart: the continent, then the territory washed in red, then a pin on the capital.
          3. The speaker button turns the music and sound effects on or off.
          4. Each round has a 40-second clock. If it runs out, the round scores 0, and the clock keeps running if you leave the page.
          5. You have three guesses. A miss costs 10 points but tells you whether your guess began too early or too late, and by how many years.
          6. In the daily you need the name itself. In Practice you can also type a country (say, “India”) to browse every answer linked to it.

          Everyone gets the same five, and a new expedition arrives at midnight. Practice has unlimited single rounds with no clock, and a 10-round run scored out of 1,000.
